Interaction with a new participant in the study

In the previous part, another participants saw how many times you reported the correct die outcome and made a decision based on that information. Now, you will be the one observing others, and see that same information about several other participants. We will call these participants "Participant 1-{{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }}"

Example:
  • 10/10 rolls means that the participant reported the exact number they saw on the screen every time.
  • 6/10 rolls means that the participant reported the exact number they saw on the screen 6 times,
    and that they did not report the correct number 4 times.
  • 0/10 rolls means that the participant never reported the exact number they saw on the screen.
{{ if treatment == 'TG' }}

Just like in Part 1 but as the other role, you are given 3 points by the computer. For each of these participants, after learning how many times they reported the correct number, you will have the opportunity to send them up to 3 points These are new points given to you by the computer in stage 2. Every point you send is tripled by the computer: 1 point sent becomes 3 points received. Then, each participant then decides how many points from those, if any, to return to you. They can return nothing, some, or all points.

Example:
  • If you send all 3 points, the other participant will receive 9 points.
  • If they return half of the 9 points, you and the Participant A will both earn 4.5 points, and will both have more than you started with.
  • If they return nothing, they will earn 9 points, while you will earn nothing.
  • So, the other participant can gain points or lose points by sending you points, depending on how much you return.

One of the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} participants you observe will be selected at random to be matched with you for the bonus. Your bonus will be the points this selected participant returns to you, and the bonus of that participant will be the points they keep.

{{ endif }} {{ if treatment == 'DG' }}

Just like in Part 1 but as the other role, you are given {{ DG_points }} by the computer. For each of these participants, after learning how many times they reported the correct number, you will have the opportunity to share these points with them. You can share nothing, some, or all the points.

Example:
  • If you share all {{ DG_points }} points, the other participant will receive {{ DG_points }} points and you receive nothing.
  • If you send {{ half_points }} points, both the other participant and yourself will earn {{ half_points }}.

One of the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} participants you observe will be selected at random to be matched with you for the bonus. Your bonus will be the points you decided to keep, if any, and the bonus of that participant will be the points you decided to share with them, if any.

{{ endif }} {{ if treatment == 'rating' }}

Just like in Part 1 but as the other role, you will rate how trustworthy you find them. You will be paid a {{ C.bonus }} bonus if your rating is the same as the most common answer for that participant.

Only of the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} participants you observe will be selected at random to be matched with you for the bonus calculation.
