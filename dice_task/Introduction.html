This study is made of three separate tasks.

  • In Part 1, you take part in a die rolling task and interact with another participant who sees the outcome of that task.
  • In Part 2, you interact with a new participant in a new task.

In each part, you have a chance to earn bonus points depending on your own decisions and that of other participants who you are matched with. 1 point in the study is worth {{ C.conversion }}.
