In this part, you will take part in a die rolling task.

  • You will roll a virtual, fair die {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} times on the computer.
  • After each roll, you are asked to report the number you rolled.
  • The number you report correspond to the number of points you will be paid for that roll.
  • 1 point is worth {{ C.conversion }}.
  • Only one of the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} dice will be randomly selected for payment.
Example:
  • If you roll a 1 and (correctly) report that you have rolled a 1, you earn £0.34,
  • If you roll a 3 and (correctly) report that you have rolled a 3, you earn £1.20,
  • If you roll a 6 and (correctly) report that you have rolled a 6, you earn £2.40,
  • If you roll a 3 and (incorrectly) report that you have rolled a 5, you earn £1.70.
{{ if player.treatment != 'control' }}

After the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} die rolls, you are matched with another participant from this same study.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'TG' }}
  • First, The other participant learns how many times you reported the die correctly.
  • Next, this participant will choose how many points to send to you.
  • Then, these points are tripled by the computer so that you get 3x as many points as they sent you.
  • Finally, you have to decide how many of those tripled points you wish to keep to yourself and how many you want to send back to the other participant, if any.

The points you kept will be paid to you as a bonus. Important: to decide how many points to send to you in the first place, the other participant will know how many times you reported the die correctly.

Example:
  • If they send all 3 points, you will receive 9 points.
  • If you return half of the 9 points, you and the other participant will both earn 4.5 points, and will both have more than you started with.
  • If you return nothing, you will earn 9 points, while they will earn nothing.

Your bonus will be the points you keep, and the bonus of the other participant will be the points you return to them.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'DG' }}
  • First, The other participant learns how many times you reported the die correctly.
  • Next, this participant is given {{ C.DG_points }}.
  • Finally, they are asked to choose how many of these points to share with you.

The points you receive from the other participant will be paid to you as a bonus. Important: to decide how many points to share with you in the first place, the other participant will know how many times you reported the die correctly.

Example:
  • If they share half the points, you will receive 3 points.
  • If they share none of the points, you will receive 0 points.
  • If they share all the points, you will receive 6 points.

Your bonus will be the points they sent you, and the bonus of the other participant will be the points they kept.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'rating' }}
  • First, The other participant learns how many times you reported the die correctly.
  • Next, this participant must rate how trustworthy you seem.

Important: to decide on your trustworthiness rating in the first place, the other participant will only know how many times you reported the die correctly.
