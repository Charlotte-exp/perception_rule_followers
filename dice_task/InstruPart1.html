{{ block title }} Instructions for Part 1 {{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

In this part, you will take part in a die rolling task.

  • You will roll a virtual, fair die {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} times on the computer.
  • After each roll, you are asked to report the number you rolled.
  • The number you report corresponds to the number of points you will be paid for that roll.
  • 1 point is worth {{ C.conversion }}.
  • Only one of the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} dice will be randomly selected to be paid as a bonus.
Example:
  • If you roll a 1 and (correctly) report that you have rolled a 1, you earn {{ one_point }}.
  • If you roll a 3 and (correctly) report that you have rolled a 3, you earn {{ three_point }}.
  • If you roll a 6 and (correctly) report that you have rolled a 6, you earn {{ six_point }}.
  • If you roll a 3 and (incorrectly) report that you have rolled a 6, you earn {{ six_point }}.
{{ if player.treatment != 'control' }}

After the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} die rolls, you are matched with another participant from this same study.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'TG' }}
  • First, the other participant receives {{ C.three_points }}.
  • Second, they learn how many times you reported the die correctly.
  • Next, this participant will choose how many of their {{ C.three_points }} to send to you.
  • Then, these points are tripled by the computer so that you get 3x as many points as they sent you.
  • Finally, you have to decide how many of those tripled points you wish to keep to yourself and how many you want to return to the other participant, if any.

Your bonus will be the points you keep, and the bonus of the other participant will be the points you return to them.

Example:
  • If they send all 3 points, you will receive 9 points.
  • If you return half of the 9 points, you and the other participant will both earn 4.5 points and will both have more than you started with.
  • If you return nothing, you will earn 9 points, while they will earn nothing.

Important: to decide how many points to send to you in the first place, the other participant will know how many times you reported the die correctly.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'DG' }}
  • First, the other participant receives {{ C.DG_points }}.
  • Next, they learn how many times you reported the die correctly.
  • Finally, this participant will choose how many of their {{ C.DG_points }} to share with you.

Your bonus will be the points the other participant shared with you.

Example:
  • If they share half the points, you will receive {{ half_points }} points.
  • If they share none of the points, you will receive 0 points.
  • If they share all the points, you will receive {{ C.DG_points }}.

Important: to decide how many points to share with you in the first place, the other participant will know how many times you reported the die correctly.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'rating' }}
  • First, the other participant learns how many times you reported the die correctly.
  • Next, this participant must rate how trustworthy you seem.

Important: to decide on your trustworthiness rating in the first place, the other participant will only know how many times you reported the die correctly.
