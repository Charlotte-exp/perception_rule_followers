In the first part, you will take part in a die rolling task.

  • You will roll a die live in the task {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} times.
  • After each roll, you must report the number you rolled.
  • The number you report is the number of points you will be paid for that roll.
  • 1 point is worth {{ C.conversion }}.
  • Only one of the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} dice will be randomly selected for payment

After the {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} die rolls, you are matched with another participant from this same study. This participant will see the number of times you reported the correct number from stage 1, and you will interact together in stage 2.

{{ if player.treatment == 'TG' }}
  • First, the other participant will choose how many points to send to you after having seen how many times you reported the correct die.
  • Then, these points are tripled by the computer so that you get 3x as much as they sent you.
  • Finally, you have to decide how many of those tripled points you wish to keep to yourself and how many you want to send back, if any.
  • The points you kept will be paid to you as the stage 2 bonus.
  • Important: to decide how many points to send to you in the first place, the other participant will only know how many times you reported the correct die.
Example:
  • If they send all 3 points, you will receive 9 points.
  • If you return half of the 9 points, you and the other participant will both earn 4.5 points, and will both have more than you started with.
  • If you return nothing, you will earn 9 points, while they will earn nothing.

Your bonus will be the points you keep, and the bonus of the other participant will be the points you return to them.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'DG' }}
  • In stage 2, the other participant is given {{ C.DG_points }} and must choose how many of these points to share with you after having seen how many times you reported the correct die.
  • The points you receive from the other participant will be paid to you as the stage 2 bonus.
  • Important: to decide how many points to share with you in the first place, the other participant will only know how many times you reported the correct die.
Example:
  • If they share half the points, you will receive 3 points.
  • If they share none of the points, you will receive 0 points.
  • If they share all of the points, you will receive 6 points.

Your bonus will be the points they sent you, and the bonus of the other participant will be the points they kept.

{{ endif }} {{ if player.treatment == 'rating' }}
  • In stage 2, the other participant must rate how trustworthy you seem after having seen how many times you reported the correct die.
  • Important: to decide how many points to send to you in the first place, the other participant will only know how many times you reported the correct die.
{{ endif }}
